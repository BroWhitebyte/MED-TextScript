**Scope:** what the US documentary record and US/English-language scholarship that cites US records say about Unit 731's material and logistical links to five sets of institutions.

**Source-type labels used throughout:**
- **(a) US RECORD** — US-produced or US-captured document, or a NARA/IWG finding aid describing one.
- **(b) JP RECORD** — Japanese official/archival record (JACAR, National Archives of Japan) or Japanese-language scholarly publication citing documents.
- **(c) CN SOURCE** — Chinese-language archive, museum, gazetteer or academic publication.

## 1. Kwantung Army Army Medical Supply Depot / 関東軍衛生材料廠

**Location question answered: FENGTIAN / MUKDEN (Shenyang), not Hsinking.** The depot also had a separate Fengtian branch depot and a Dalian branch office.

- **(b) JP RECORD** — JACAR Ref. **C13010198500**, 「第４篇・第３章／第２節　満洲の国防軍需産業の特質と兵站との関係（２）」, from 防衛省防衛研究所 (National Institute for Defense Studies), series 「満洲に於ける各種作戦の史的観察　第４篇　第３章　兵站　昭和７～１６年」. Content field gives 「関東軍衛生材料廠」: **(1) 所在地 満洲国奉天市十港屯; 敷地 約四十万坪; 機能及設備 衛生材料の製造、補給並に貯蔵**. In-unit plant (製薬工場, ~2,000+ tsubo) made 食塩注射液 (saline injection), ブドー糖ビタミンB液 (glucose/vitamin B solution) and tablets (健胃錠, 食塩錠, 重曹錠, ビタミン錠, クレオソート丸). It **also contracted annual production to civilian pharmaceutical factories in the Fengtian area**, stored the material in unit warehouses, and supplied it to **全満各陸軍病院 (all Manchurian Army hospitals), 野戦貨物廠 (関東軍及中北支軍) (field depots of the Kwantung Army and the North/Central China Army) and 在満各部隊 (units in Manchuria)**. Physical plant: 事務所(部隊本部) ~1,000 tsubo, 修理工場 ~2,000 tsubo, 倉庫大小約40棟; a section (2) 出張所 (branch offices) exists but the catalogue text is truncated. → https://www.jacar.archives.go.jp/das/meta/C13010198500

## 2. South Manchuria Railway Co. (Mantetsu / 満鉄)

**The single best-documented Mantetsu→BW link is the Mantetsu Hygiene Research Institute at Dalian, taken over by the Kwantung Army in 1938 and placed under Unit 731.** A second, separately documented Mantetsu body is the **Fengtian Epizootic Research Institute**, connected in Japanese scholarship to Unit 100.

- **(c) CN SOURCE** — 半島晨報, 「731部队魔爪曾经伸进大连」, material supplied by 中共大连市委党史研究室 (CPC Dalian Municipal Party History Research Office). Chronology: Mantetsu opened 1907; in 1924 **金井章次 (Kanai Shōji)**, head of the Mantetsu Local Department hygiene section, proposed a hygiene research institute; **March 1925** the colonial authorities forced 17 Chinese households off 13 mu of vegetable/cotton land at 大连下葭町 and began construction; completed December 1925; **operations began January 1926**, 金井章次 concurrently director, 30 staff (20 Japanese, 10 Chinese labourers), **mainly producing smallpox vaccine (牛痘苗) for the Japanese Army, Mantetsu personnel and Japanese immigrants**; **April 1927 renamed 満鉄衛生研究所** (Mantetsu Hygiene Research Institute), expanding into measles, plague and scarlet-fever research and serum/vaccine development. After 1931 金井 resigned; **安東洪次 (Andō Kōji)** later became director. By **1937** the site was 38,135 m² with 6,120 m² of buildings in 41 structures and **seven sections**: 細菌, 病理, 化学, 衛生, 血清, 痘苗, 庶務. **1938:** after 731 HQ moved to Pingfang, the Kwantung Army seized the Mantetsu institute citing "military need for serum and vaccine", placed it **under direct Unit 731 control**, and renamed it externally **大連衛生研究所**. The supply relationship was defined **both ways**: if the Dalian institute's serum raw-material supply fell short, **Unit 731 HQ would make it up**; when Unit 731 HQ and its detachments needed serum or vaccine, **the Dalian institute would supply it**. Also: 1943 order by 軍医少尉 黒木正彦 to research gas-gangrene (壊疽) vaccine; Unit 731 HQ repeatedly sent technicians, incl. cholera specialist 湊正雄 with the entire bacteriology section and most of the insect section, bringing 4 ultrasonic devices; the institute held **500–600 "石井式細菌培養罐" (Ishii-type bacterial culture tanks)**. → https://www.sohu.com/a/936077905_349247
- **(c) CN SOURCE** — 大連日報, 「731细菌部队在大连活动始末」 (reprinted by 东北新闻网, Liaoning). Adds: Unit 731 had "8 departments, 7 BW units, 1 institute", the institute being the one at Dalian; director **安東洪次**, later promoted lieutenant-general; internally "**関東軍防疫給水部大連出張所**", also "**満洲第319部隊**"; predecessor 大連満鉄衛生研究所 founded **August 1925** (this date differs from 半島晨報's 1925–26 chronology — flag the discrepancy); in **1939** Ishii moved most of the Harbin core staff to the Dalian Mantetsu institute; whenever 731 HQ and detachments needed vaccine, this institute guaranteed supply; approx. 250 personnel. Testimony: **山内豊紀** (assistant to 731 medical major 渡辺道) stated that at surrender **500+ culture tanks were dumped into the deep sea off Dalian** because there was no time to dispose of them. **1954-08-11** statement of former 関東州庁警察部長 **潮海辰亥**: in Feb–Mar 1944 the Harbin Ishii bacterial unit's subordinate Dalian bacterial research institute asked him to approve the use of a living person from 西崛红十字医院 for a bacterial experiment; he recalled approving once. **1942:** Kwantung Army **693部隊** built a factory at 金州龍王廟 nominally an "Army Hospital" but in fact for bacterial research/production, with 8,000 labourers dying there. Post-war: Soviet troops took over part; 1946–47 the institute resumed smallpox/typhoid/cholera vaccine production; May 1949 taken over by the CPC 旅大 organisation; **1950 renamed 大連生物製品研究所**, under the Ministry of Health. → https://liaoning.nen.com.cn/network/liaoningnews/lnnewslndangan/2018/07/12/252446951012832266.shtml
- **(b) JP RECORD / Japanese scholarship** — **小河孝（Ogawa Takashi）著『満州における軍馬の鼻疽と関東軍：奉天獣疫研究所・馬疫研究処・100部隊』、文理閣、2020年3月、10+123 pp、ISBN 978-4-89259-861-6.** Library catalogue annotation: 「満州における「鼻疽」の研究と防疫対策を分析。**奉天獣疫研究所、馬疫研究処、１００部隊（関東軍軍馬防疫廠）**の設立の背景、鼻疽に関する研究と防疫活動、関係者を含む**各機関の相互のつながり**など、実態を解き明かす。」 — i.e. a Japanese monograph whose stated purpose is to elucidate the **mutual connections** among the **Fengtian Epizootic Research Institute (奉天獣疫研究所)**, the **Horse Epizootic Research Station (馬疫研究処)** and **Unit 100 (関東軍軍馬防疫廠)**. This is the strongest verified Mantetsu-affiliated-research-body → BW-unit link in the Japanese literature, but the monograph's interior evidence is not online. → http://www.lib.pref.miyazaki.lg.jp/winj/opac/switch-detail.do?lang=ja&bibid=1102340414 ; also catalogued at https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB30761492

- **(a) US / English scholarship** — **Johnson (2021), *Journal of the History of Medicine and Allied Sciences* 77(1): 24–47, doi:10.1093/jhmas/jrab044**, cites captured Soviet documents and Harris: Unit 731's airborne trials took place "at **Anta railway station**, also known as Unit 731's 'proving grounds'"; and the Soviet-captured documents included "**a waybill for train freight** that described the transport of '**officers and lower ranks totaling 40, with equipment (secret weapons and material)**'", with the disguise name "**Nara Unit**". → https://academic.oup.com/jhmas/article/77/1/24/6460153 (full text extracted locally from `dl/04_Johnson_2021_JHMAS_Unit-731_Human-Experimentation.pdf`)
