沦陷区殖民统治研究
出版时间： 1995 
导师： 张静如教授指导 
学位授予单位： 北京师范大学 
论文作者： 夏潮著 
副题名： 
学科专业： 中共党史

中文摘要 
本文是对中国沦陷区社会殖民统治的研究。 本文的引言部分论述了这一研究的学术意义和现实意义，并对研究的具体内容、范围及重点作了说明。 本文主文共分三编。 第一编是对沦陷区殖民统治的经济基础――沦陷区社会经济状况的考察。第―编共三章。第一章和第二章分别考察了东北沦陷区殖民地型经济和关内沦陷区殖民掠夺性经济的状况。重点对日本殖民统治者在沦陷区的经济政策、掠夺方式以及日本垄断资本在沦陷区社会各经济部门垄断和统制的程度进行了分析。同时也考察了东北沦陷区残存的民族资本主义以及关内沦陷区挣扎生存的民族资本主义的状况。 第三章对沦陷区时期殖民社会里一种特殊型态的“国家”垄断资本主义――汪伪统制经济的性质、特点、主要内容以及在殖民地经济统治中的地位进行了考察。 第二编是对沦陷区殖民地社会政治权力结构的综合考察。通过这一考察，分析和探讨了沦陷区殖民主义政治统治的构成和特点。第二编共五章。第四章考察了军队这一权力系统在沦陷区社会政治权力结构中的地位及职能。第五章考察了沦陷区宪警特三位一体的特务政治的功能以及在殖民统治中的地位。第六章系统分析了沦陷区伪政权系统的组成、结构、社会控制功能以及与中国旧有行政控制系统的关系，并专门考察了沦陷区基层权力控制系统――保甲系统的殖民地特色。第七章对沦陷区所特有的准权力系统――协和会、新民会、东亚联盟总会系统在社会政治权力结构中的地位和职能进行了考察。第八章对本不属社会权力系统但因依附于社会权力系统而具有权力功能的汉奸帮会和土匪武装进行了考察。 第三编是对沦陷区的社会教化和社会规范控制机制的考察。通过这一考察，探究了沦陷区社会殖民主义思想文化统治的特点。第三编共四章。第九章对殖民统治者利用封建礼教与宗教训导、教化、规范、约束沦陷区人民的情况进行了考察。第十章对沦陷区社会教化的一个重要特点――思想教化运动的持续开展进行了研究。第十一章是对沦陷区新闻传媒和舆论的统制的考察。第十二章是对沦陷区殖民地奴化教育的考察。 本文结语部分提出了沦陷区殖民统治与一般宗主国总督制殖民地统治以及割地租让式殖民统治相比所具有的四个特点。即：以经济掠夺为核心内容的殖民统治；以军权为核心支撑的殖民统治；以训练“顺民”为最终目标的殖民统治以及统治形式极具欺编性的殖民统治。

英文摘要 
ABSTRACT The paper is focused on an overall investigation and study of the situations and its characteristics of the social colonial domination in the enemy―occupied area in Old China. Its introduction discusses the academic significance and practical significance of the research work, and expounds its concrete contents, renge and key points. The paper is divided into three chapters. The first chapter is an investigation on the economic base of the colonial domination in the enemy―occupied area――the social economic situation in the enemy―occupied area. The first chapter consists of three sections. The first and the second section investigate respectively the colonial type economy in the enemy―occupied area in the Northeast and the colonial plundering economy of that area inside Shanhaiguan Pass. Focuses on analyzing Japanese colonialist's economic policy, methods of plundering in the enemy―occupied area and its monopoly and governing degrees by Japanese monopoly capitalism in the various economic departments in the society of the enemy―occupied area, and the situation of the surviving national capitalism who was struggling to live in the area inside Shanhaiguan Pass is investigated simultaneously. The third section focuses on the nature, characteristics and main contents of a special type"state"monopoly capitalism in the colonial society during the enemy-occupied period――Wang puppet governing economy and its position kin the colonial economic control. The second chapter is a comprehensive investigation to the political power in the colonial society of the enemy―occupied area. To analyse and discuss its forming and characteristics of the colonialist political governing through the investigation. The second chapter consists of five sections. The fourth section focuses on the position and governing function of the authority system of army in the construction of the social political power in the enemy―occupied area. The fifth section analyses the function of the secret agent politics of three in one of the military police, the police, and the secret agent in the enemy―occupied area. The sixth section analyses systematically the formation, structure and social control function of the puppet regime in the enemy―occupied area, and specifically investigate the colonial characteristics of the basic authority control system――the Bao―Jia system in the enemy―occupied area. The seventh section does an investigation to the function of the peculiar authority system in the enemy―occupied area of Xuehe（协和）Association, Xinmin（新民）Association and the Federation of East Asian Alliance system, and their position in the social political power construction. The eighth section does a survey to those secret societies of traitors and the armed bandits who do not belong to social power system but attached to social power system and possessing political power function. The third chapter is an investigation into the control system of social inculcating and social standard in the enemy―occupied area, so as to explore the characteristics of social colonialist thought and culture of that area. The third chapter consists four sections. The ninth section is an investigation into the state of how the colonialist took the feudal etiquette and religious teaching to instruct, inculcate, standadize and restrain people in the enemy―occupied area. The tenth section is a study on an important characteristic of the social inculcating movement in the enemy―occupied area. The eleventh chapter is an investigation to the control of the news media and the public opinion in the enemy―occupied area. The twelfth chapter is an investigation to the colonial enslaving education in the area. The conclusion of this paper puts forward four characteristics from the comparative study in colonial control in the enemy―occupied area with the colonial control of governor―general in a metropolitan state and the colonial control by ceding territory and renting, they are: the colonial control with key contents of economic plundering; the colonial control with key support of military power; the colonial control with final target of training"tractable people"and the colonial control with its formality of extremely deceitful.
